BOARD GAME ARENA DEVELOPMENT

Jade Stone Merchants

A published auction card game, adapted for Board Game Arena.

Preserving the original rules and artwork, with a readable shared board, server-validated actions, automatic scoring and card movement that makes each turn easy to follow.

Working implementation in BGA Studio. Private-alpha deployment has been requested.

What the adaptation includes

  • A PHP rules engine with server-side legal-action checks.
  • Two to five players, automatic scoring and spectator support.
  • Reload recovery, BGA replay controls and native sound settings.
  • A responsive board built around the original card artwork.

Completed Studio tests cover two to five players. The PHP and TypeScript rules engines were compared across 96 complete games.
